# Audience learning after authorized release

This plan is not permission to post, contact viewers, or schedule monitoring. “Viral” is not a workflow status. Record intended audience, objective, comparison group, measurement date, and exact export version. Internal tests cannot establish best possible performance.

| Question | Evidence | Possible hypothesis, not a diagnosis by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Did people choose to watch? | Shorts viewed-versus-swiped/stayed-to-watch, feed exposure, first moments | Opening unclear, unappealing, or mismatched to audience |
| Did interest persist? | Retention by time, average view duration/percentage | Particular explanation, stall, confusing join, or premature answer loses interest |
| Did payoff satisfy? | End retention, relevant feedback, available satisfaction signals | Ending rewards the promise or fails to resolve it |
| Did people value passing it on? | Available shares/sends and actual comments | Particular recognition, surprise, tenderness, or delight encourages sharing |

YouTube Shorts average view duration/percentage use engaged views; distinguish them from the initial decision to stay. A replay spike can indicate interest or confusion. Do not equate raw views across platforms or changing view definitions.

## Compare fairly

- Prefer the channel's similar format, genre/audience, length, publication age, and traffic source. Record sample size and uncertainty.
- Without a baseline, call early releases exploratory. Do not invent thresholds such as “80% retention guarantees distribution.”
- Audience matching, competition, topic interest, and seasonality also affect reach. Uneven organic exposure is not a controlled experiment.
- Keep version history; do not repeatedly delete/repost merely to chase a preferred result.

## Test one useful revision

Record observation → scene/timecode → alternative explanations → proposed change → expected result → actual result. A different story, title, opening, and mix together cannot isolate one cause. Use focused variants only when they can answer a worthwhile question; do not promise built-in A/B testing the platform does not offer.

Choose honest titles/covers. Separate Shorts-feed first-frame appeal from title/thumbnail discovery elsewhere. Preserve resolution rather than manufacture a cliffhanger for replay. Refresh official platform definitions before applying this plan; sources in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).
