# Hooked Short Film Workflow

A reusable, research-informed workflow for original narrative films **strictly under 60 seconds**, in any genre or art style. Designed for VideoExpress production through the in-app browser.

Start with [SYSTEM_PROMPT.md](SYSTEM_PROMPT.md). It leads the producer through story development, visual planning, prompting, generation, editing, and delivery. Default: a standalone 9:16 film aiming for 45–55 seconds, with a resolved central dramatic question and an earned emotional ending.

## Use

Paste this into your assistant and supply the repository URL:

> Read SYSTEM_PROMPT.md and its linked workflow files in this repository. Use it to produce an original film in VideoExpress through the in-app browser. Topic: [premise]. Genre: [genre]. Art style: [style]. Target length: [45–55 seconds; always under 60]. Proceed through development and production without routine approval pauses. Preserve existing versions. Deliver a separate review export; do not publish publicly.

If only writing or planning is requested, stop at that scope. Creating this repository does not authorize spending credits on a film.

## Files

- [SYSTEM_PROMPT.md](SYSTEM_PROMPT.md): operating instructions and production sequence.
- [PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md](PROMPT_TEMPLATES.md): first-frame, motion/audio, and repair prompts.
- [VIDEOEXPRESS.md](VIDEOEXPRESS.md): browser production adapter and recovery.
- [PRODUCTION_TEMPLATE.md](PRODUCTION_TEMPLATE.md): reusable film plan and scene ledger.
- [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md): genre and style adaptations; worked short-film plan.
- [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md): sources, limitations, and design rationale.
- [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md): version history.

These are production recommendations, not official VideoExpress documentation or a promise of retention, virality, or artistic success. Instructions for app controls are starting points that must be reconciled with the visible interface. Version 1.0 has not yet been validated through an end-to-end film production.
