## The production path

1. Define audience and emotional promise.
2. Compare different premise/opening/payoff combinations and reject weak ones.
3. Map what viewers know, expect, and still want answered through the story.
4. Write the complete timed picture-and-sound script.
5. Complete VISUAL_DEVELOPMENT.md: compare three distinct visual opening designs, select two for rough playback, define memorable imagery and visual progression, annotate each storyboard panel, and plan a picture-led payoff; maintain voice/performance/continuity.
6. Compare opening variants in a complete rough sequence; gather fresh-viewer feedback when available/authorized.
7. Present the development package for review by default; generate polished footage only within authorization.
8. Edit actual performances, then separately review creative execution and technical export requirements.
9. Deliver a review version; publish only when authorized and learn from real audience results.

## Required film-development review package

Before a default production run proceeds, present the selected premise and rejected alternatives; clear central question and payoff; obvious-solution findings; complete timed narration/dialogue/picture script; viewer information map; three distinct visual opening concepts and selection reasons; story-specific memorable visual idea; progression map; panel-level interest/anticipation answers; visual payoff/setup; storyboard/opening comparison; rough playback status and findings; fresh-viewer results or SELF-REVIEW ONLY; and proposed fixes/remaining uncertainties. The user reviews a concrete film plan rather than a general promise.

## v2.2 performance and finish revision — 2026-10-24

Added PERFORMANCE_AND_FINISH.md and connected it to the controlling workflow, visual development, prompts, adapter, tests, and production record. The prior version specified desired qualities without enough execution evidence. Required additions are directed opening narration comparisons and a timed full delivery; acting/design references; phrase-to-picture timing; every-join records and playback; a dedicated pacing pass; a representative finished two-shot method test before bulk generation; and actual mix/color/motion review before creative approval.

Document-level cases reviewed for this revision:
- Intelligible but flat narration: consistency alone is insufficient; compare actual opening performances and direct the complete delivery.
- Matching boundary stills but an awkward moving cut: inspect the join in normal-speed sound-on context.
- A five-second source with idle remainder: retain only developing action/meaning; compare a tighter cut when a stall is found.
- Polished image with drifting face or floating gift: inspect motion and repair the material defect.
- Correct words with an early solution reveal: inspect timed phrase/picture evidence and restore the intended uncertainty.
- Two attractive isolated shots with an untested narrator or join: complete the integrated representative method sample before bulk footage.
- Autonomous authorization with unavailable sample playback: development may continue, but bulk polished generation remains pending.
- Intentionally unnarrated comedy or graphic animation: narrator fields may be N/A; retain performance, story, timing, continuity, and finish checks appropriate to the medium.

At the time of the v2.2 document revision, these were documentation-level rule checks, not executed audiovisual trials or fresh-viewer tests; no film or quality sample had yet been produced under that revision. Disney-quality craft remains the requested benchmark, not demonstrated equivalence.

## v2.3: what the completed film exposed

The user judged the completed Christmas edit acceptable but a little too choppy. Its actual local edit ledger contains nonuniform shot retiming, linked silence removal from sound and picture, and a cropped continuation cut. Those facts support investigating interrupted motion and compressed rhythm; they do not establish which boundary caused the user's response without a new moving/sound-on comparison. No changes to that film are authorized for this revision.

The prior instructions already rejected universal cut intervals and requested per-join playback. More general language would not solve the execution gap. SMOOTH_EDITING.md now specifies a concrete order and disallowed shortcuts: independent speech cleanup, complete native-speed action assembly, event anchors rather than phrase-boundary matching, justified coverage and sound bridges, breathing-room comparison for rushed passages, caption clearance throughout motion, and continuous exact-export review. The two-shot test is retained and supplemented by a connected dramatic sequence; it is not multiplied into repeated auditions or new approvals for already accepted assets.

The new regression cases in STORY_TESTS.md are documentation-level rules. A future production must record actual outcomes before any smoothness claim. Technical decode and ASR do not supply that evidence. The default GitHub branch previously held v1 while the revised documents lived on workflow-v2-review; v2.3 makes the same current instructions accessible from both paths and keeps the prior history recoverable.
